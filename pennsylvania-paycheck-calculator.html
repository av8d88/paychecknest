How much tax do you pay in Pennsylvania?

Pennsylvania has a flat state income tax of 3.07%, applied the same way to all taxable income regardless of how much you earn.

A single filer earning $75,000 in Pennsylvania takes home roughly $59,290 per year after federal tax, state tax and FICA (estimate, 2026 rules, no other deductions).

Pennsylvania income tax at a glance

  • State income tax: 3.07% flat state income tax
  • Federal standard deduction (2026): $16,100 single / $32,200 married filing jointly
  • Social Security: 6.2% on wages up to $184,500
  • Medicare: 1.45% on all wages (+0.9% over $200,000)

Frequently asked questions

Does Pennsylvania have a state income tax?

Pennsylvania has a flat state income tax of 3.07%, applied the same way to all taxable income regardless of how much you earn.

How accurate is this calculator?

It models 2026 federal brackets, FICA and Pennsylvania state brackets. Real withholding also depends on W-4 settings, benefits, local taxes and credits, so treat the result as a close planning estimate, not tax advice.

What is the "no tax on overtime / tips" deduction?

Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (2025–2028), eligible workers can deduct up to $12,500 of overtime premium pay ($25,000 for joint filers) and up to $25,000 of tips from federal taxable income. Try our overtime tax calculator and tips tax calculator.
