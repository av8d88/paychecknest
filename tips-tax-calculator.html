How the "no tax on tips" deduction works

Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (tax years 2025–2028), workers in tipped occupations can deduct up to $25,000 of qualified tips from federal taxable income.

  • Applies to voluntarily paid tips (cash, card, or tip-sharing)
  • Reduces federal income tax only — FICA still applies to all tips
  • Phases out above $150,000 single / $300,000 joint modified AGI
  • Available whether or not you itemize deductions

Example: $15,000 of tips in the 12% bracket saves about $1,800 in federal tax.
