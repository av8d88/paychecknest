What does $25/hour look like after taxes in Kentucky?

At $25/hour, 40 hours a week, your gross pay is $52,000 a year. As a single filer in Kentucky you'd take home roughly $42,258 per year (2026 rules). Kentucky charges a flat 3.50% state income tax.

How hourly pay is taxed in Kentucky

  • Your gross pay = hourly wage × hours per week × 52
  • Federal income tax uses 2026 brackets after the $16,100 standard deduction (single)
  • Social Security takes 6.2% (up to $184,500 of wages) and Medicare 1.45%
  • Kentucky charges a flat 3.50% state income tax.
