How the "no tax on overtime" deduction works

Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (tax years 2025–2028), eligible workers can deduct the overtime premium portion of their pay — the extra "half" of time-and-a-half — from federal taxable income.

  • Maximum deduction: $12,500 (single) or $25,000 (married filing jointly)
  • Only the premium portion (0.5x) qualifies, not your full overtime pay
  • Reduces federal income tax only — Social Security and Medicare still apply
  • Phases out at higher incomes ($150,000 single / $300,000 joint, modified AGI)

Example: at $25/hour with 8 overtime hours a week, your annual deductible premium is $25 × 0.5 × 8 × 52 = $5,200. In the 22% bracket that saves about $1,144 in federal tax.
